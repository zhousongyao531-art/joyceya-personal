Xiaohongshu Node Marketing

Bananain Double 12 Campaign

Insight & Goal

At the Double 12 peak, winter basics faced intense category competition. The goal was to translate warmth, comfort, antibacterial fabric, and lightweight wear into searchable Xiaohongshu language and build trust before conversion.

Strategy & Rules

I designed a three-stage rhythm - pre-heat, seeding, and conversion - supported by KOL/KOC tiers, content formats, keyword clusters, trial rules, and publishing-frequency guidelines.

Delivery & Monitoring

The plan broke delivery into creator selection, briefing, content review, scheduled publishing, and review. Key risks included repetitive content, excessive advertising tone, timing conflicts, and negative feedback.

Output & Validation

The final deck provides a complete node-marketing playbook. Its evaluation funnel covers reach, engagement rate, keyword search, saves, store visits, and conversion; actual campaign results should be added only when verified.

My Role

Independent planning project. I completed platform insight, creator tiering, operating rules, publishing rhythm, delivery checklist, risk controls, and the measurement framework.

小红书节点营销

蕉内双十二营销方案

洞察与目标

在双十二流量高峰与冬季保暖品类竞争下，项目需要将保暖、轻薄、舒适等产品功能转化为用户可搜索、可讨论的内容语言，并在转化前建立产品信任。

策略与规则

设计“预热—种草—集中转化”三阶段运营节奏，并建立 KOL/KOC 分层、内容类型、关键词矩阵、试用机制和发布频次规则。

推进与监控

将执行拆分为达人筛选、Brief 沟通、内容审核、节点发布与数据复盘；重点控制内容同质化、广告感过强、发布时间冲突和负面反馈风险。

产出与验证

形成完整的小红书节点营销方案，评估链路覆盖曝光、互动率、关键词搜索、收藏、进店和转化；仅在真实执行数据确认后补充业务结果。

我的职责

个人独立策划，完成平台洞察、达人分层、内容规则、发布节奏、执行清单、风险预案和指标体系设计。

IP Campaign Planning

Zero Point Idol: OUYE Coconut Milk

Insight & Goal

The coconut-drink category is highly homogeneous, while younger users increasingly value health, affordability, and emotional connection. The goal was to create a memorable IP without allowing the concept to obscure product benefits.

Strategy & Rules

The "Zero Point Idol" idea personified the coconut ingredient as Ouo and translated production into an idol-growth story. Each campaign stage paired one narrative beat with one product proof and one user action.

Delivery & Monitoring

The plan organized IP design, social topics, festival collaboration, offline interaction, documentary content, and live-stream ideas by milestone. Monitoring focused on message consistency, IP recognition, product visibility, and cross-channel timing.

Output & Validation

The output is an online-offline IP operating framework. Suggested indicators include IP recognition, topic participation, UGC volume, live engagement, offline participation, and product conversion.

My Role

Independent planning project. I developed the audience insight, IP concept, stage rules, activation structure, risk checks, and measurement path.

IP 营销策划

零分偶像：OU椰椰汁

洞察与目标

椰汁品类同质化明显，年轻用户同时关注健康、性价比和情绪价值。项目目标是建立有记忆点的 IP，同时避免创意概念遮蔽产品卖点。

策略与规则

提出“零分偶像”概念，将椰子拟人化为角色“欧欧”，把生产过程转译为偶像成长叙事；每个传播阶段对应一个剧情节点、一个产品证据和一个用户动作。

推进与监控

按里程碑组织 IP 设定、社交话题、音乐节合作、线下互动、纪录片内容和直播玩法，持续检查人设一致性、产品露出、渠道节奏和执行依赖。

产出与验证

形成线上线下联动的 IP 运营框架，建议通过 IP 认知、话题参与、UGC 数量、直播互动、线下参与和产品转化进行评估。

我的职责

个人独立策划，完成用户洞察、IP 概念、阶段规则、活动架构、风险检查和指标路径设计。

Qunar x Divas Hit the Road

Insight & Goal

The travel-show IP carried strong emotional memory, but attention alone did not guarantee platform action. The goal was to turn viewing interest into route discovery, search, comparison, and booking intent.

Strategy & Rules

Celebrity routes, friendship moments, and destination scenes became travel triggers in a funnel from content exposure to route saves, platform search, product pages, and booking.

Delivery & Monitoring

The project was split into IP coordination, content publishing, route packaging, in-app landing, and interaction. Risks included a gap between show buzz and available products, inconsistent landing content, and schedule delays.

Output & Validation

The deck delivered the co-brand theme, social content, route inspiration, platform conversion, and interactive mechanics. Suggested indicators include engagement, route saves, branded search, detail-page visits, and booking conversion.

My Role

Independent planning project. I designed the co-brand logic, user journey, content matrix, platform handoff, milestones, and measurement framework.

产出与验证

形成联名主题、社交内容、旅行路线、站内承接和互动玩法方案，建议监控内容互动、路线收藏、品牌搜索、详情页访问和预订转化。

我的职责

个人独立策划，完成联名逻辑、用户路径、内容矩阵、平台承接、项目节点和评估指标设计。

Cultural Tourism Campaign

Shibadong Village x Spring Festival

Insight & Goal

Spring Festival offers high emotional relevance, while Shibadong Village combines rural revitalization, local culture, and destination value. The goal was to turn cultural narrative into an experience users could understand, share, and visit.

Strategy & Rules

Reunion, local customs, and village stories formed the core narrative. Content, cultural touchpoints, and routes were organized around a single festival theme with clear visitor participation paths.

Delivery & Monitoring

The plan separated content production, route preparation, on-site experience, local coordination, and visitor communication. Risk controls covered cultural accuracy, public sentiment, crowd flow, weather, and on-site safety.

Output & Validation

The output includes a campaign theme, cultural touchpoints, route ideas, communication content, and experience packaging. Suggested indicators include reach, saves, route-page visits, participation, travel intent, and visitor feedback.

My Role

Independent planning project. I completed the cultural research, experience journey, campaign structure, milestone plan, risk checklist, and evaluation path.

The brand needed to challenge the category perception of high sugar and heavy coloring while strengthening healthier ingredients, minimalist aesthetics, and premium positioning for women aged 24-35.

Strategy & Rules

"Hidden Blue" became the shared visual and emotional cue connecting ritual moments, refined taste, and lifestyle needs across content, media, and product expression.

Delivery & Monitoring

The project was split into audience insight, concept validation, content production, media allocation, budget planning, and review. Controls focused on compliant health claims, visual consistency, and message alignment.

Output & Validation

The deck delivered TA analysis, communication theme, content strategy, budget framework, and KPI estimates. The measurement model links brand awareness, engagement, store visits, enquiries, and purchase conversion.

My Role

Independent planning project. I completed category and audience insight, positioning, campaign concept, project structure, risk controls, and the KPI framework.

Planning Reflection

Across these independent planning cases, I treat creativity as one part of an operating system. My process begins with industry and user insight, then defines rules, milestones, dependencies, risk controls, and measurement indicators so that a campaign can move from an idea toward executable delivery and accountable review.
