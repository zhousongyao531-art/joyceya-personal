Commercial AIGC Tool

Commercial Text-to-Image AI Agent

Insight & Goal

Advertising teams need many visual concepts across industries, platforms, sizes, and conversion goals. The goal was to shorten first-round exploration while reducing fragmented prompts and dependence on repeated design requests.

Strategy & Rules

I organized the workflow around 5 creative scenarios and standardized 4 reusable components: industry templates, negative prompts, ratio rules, and a case library. The structure turns individual prompting experience into repeatable operating rules.

Delivery & Monitoring

Results are reviewed for brand consistency, text accuracy, visual compliance, copyright risk, and scenario fit. Failed cases return to the prompt template and case library so that errors become inputs for the next iteration.

Output & Validation

The current prototype covers 5 commercial creative scenarios: search ads, feed ads, landing-page visuals, video covers, and campaign posters. Future validation should track time per first draft, first-pass acceptance, prompt reuse, and design rework rather than claiming efficiency without usage data.

My Role

Independent planning and AI-assisted build. I completed scenario analysis, rule design, prompt schema, case-library planning, quality checks, and workflow testing.

AI Marketing Intelligence

AI Marketing X Report

Insight & Goal

AI marketing information changes quickly and is scattered across sources. The goal was to help teams distinguish useful industry signals from noise and turn them into material for proposals, client conversations, and campaign planning.

Strategy & Rules

I built a 4-stage workflow - collection, topic scoring, insight synthesis, and delivery - and used timeliness, industry relevance, business value, and actionability as the review logic for prioritizing topics.

Delivery & Monitoring

Source records and judgment notes are retained while duplicate, low-credibility, and low-value signals are filtered. Human review checks citations, context, and conclusions to reduce hallucination and overgeneralization.

Output & Validation

The workflow produces 2 reusable formats: PPT outlines and client-facing talking points. Continued use should be evaluated through production time per issue, content adoption, reuse frequency, and business-use count.

My Role

Independent planning and AI-assisted build. I defined the product positioning, source workflow, scoring logic, editorial structure, output templates, and review controls.

Data Monitoring Dashboard

Customer Churn Dashboard

Insight & Goal

Customer movement, revenue impact, and industry distribution were difficult to review in one place. The goal was to identify anomalies earlier and establish a consistent path from risk detection to follow-up and review.

Strategy & Rules

I organized the dashboard around 5 operating dimensions and structured the workflow as identify, assess, prioritize, follow up, and review. Risk levels and ownership can be added once production thresholds and team responsibilities are confirmed.

Delivery & Monitoring

The design defines metric meaning, update cadence, privacy controls, and a review sequence. AI can support anomaly explanation and action suggestions, while final risk decisions remain with the operator.

Output & Validation

One dashboard consolidates 5 operating dimensions: customer profile, customer-count movement, revenue impact, IMS involvement, and industry ranking. Future validation should track high-risk customers identified, follow-up time, recovery outcomes, and churn-rate movement; the prototype does not claim a reduction by itself.

My Role

Independent planning and prototype build. I defined the metrics, information hierarchy, review path, privacy treatment, risk logic, and follow-up framework.

数据监测看板

客户流失看板

洞察与目标

客户数量、金额影响和行业分布信息难以集中查看，团队容易在结果滞后后才发现问题。 项目目标是更早识别异常，并建立从风险发现到跟进复盘的统一路径。

策略与规则

围绕 5 个运营维度组织看板，并将工作流拆分为“识别—判断—排序—跟进—复盘”； 待接入真实业务后再根据阈值和团队职责配置风险等级与负责人。

推进与监控

明确指标口径、更新节奏、隐私控制和阅读顺序；AI 用于辅助异常解释与动作建议， 最终风险判断和客户跟进仍由运营人员确认。

产出与验证

以 1 个看板集中呈现客户基本信息、客户数量变化、金额影响、IMS 参与、行业排名 5 个运营维度， 建立统一的问题排查路径。后续通过高风险客户识别数、跟进时效、挽回结果和流失率变化验证价值， 当前原型不直接宣称流失率下降。

我的职责

个人独立策划与原型搭建，完成指标设计、信息层级、阅读路径、数据脱敏、风险逻辑和跟进框架。

AI Search Advertising Tool

Keyword Expansion Assistant Iteration

Insight & Goal

Generic expansion tools produce duplicated, low-intent, and regionally irrelevant keywords, leaving operators with heavy manual cleanup. The goal was to turn search advertising experience into rules that improve media readiness and review efficiency.

Strategy & Rules

The workflow contains 8 modules: search suggestions, industry recognition, commercial-intent filtering, negative keywords, match recommendations, quality prediction, region locking, and East China regional drill-down.

Delivery & Monitoring

Iteration followed four stages: expansion, cleanup, quality assessment, and regional adaptation. Reviews focus on duplicates, intent errors, location drift, weak relevance, and conflicts between suggested keywords and landing pages.

Output & Validation

The live prototype organizes each result through 6 decision fields: source, intent, match recommendation, quality forecast, relevance score, and delivery advice. Production validation should track usable-keyword rate, cleanup time, adoption, and campaign ROI.

My Role

Independent planning and AI-assisted build. I completed requirement analysis, rule design, evaluation fields, iteration management, testing, and interface refinement.

AI 搜索广告工具

搜索广告拓词助手迭代

洞察与目标

通用拓词工具容易产生重复词、弱商业意图词和地域偏差，运营人员仍需投入大量时间二次筛选。 项目目标是把搜索投放经验沉淀为规则，提升结果的投放可用性和审核效率。

策略与规则

将拓词流程拆分为搜索联想、行业识别、商业意图筛选、否词过滤、匹配方式推荐、质量度预判、 地域锁定和华东地域下钻 8 个规则模块。

推进与监控

按“基础拓词—结果清洗—质量评估—地域适配”分阶段迭代，重点监控重复词、意图误判、 地域漂移、低相关性结果和关键词与落地页不一致风险。

产出与验证

线上原型 通过来源、用户意图、匹配建议、质量度预测、相关性评分和投放建议 6 个字段组织结果。 后续结合真实投放数据验证有效词率、筛选耗时、采用率和 ROI。

我的职责

个人独立策划与 AI 辅助搭建，完成需求分析、规则设计、评估字段、迭代管理、测试和界面优化。

AI Sports Data Dashboard

2026 World Cup Real-Time Dashboard

Insight & Goal

With 48 teams, 12 groups, and dense schedules, users face fragmented match, standings, odds, and prediction information. The goal was to create one scan-friendly entry point for tracking and deeper exploration.

Strategy & Rules

The product combines teams, groups, schedule, match details, odds, predictions, and a Claude assistant in one information architecture. A rolling 9-day window and 30-minute group-data refresh define the core operating rules.

Delivery & Monitoring

Delivery was split into data integration, standings logic, interaction, prediction, and bilingual experience. Monitoring covers API failure, data freshness, rule consistency, prediction error, and API-key security; keys remain in the local browser.

Output & Validation

The live dashboard covers 48 teams, 12 group tables, a 9-day schedule window, and 30-minute refreshes. Validation focuses on functional coverage, refresh stability, information readability, and later prediction back-testing rather than unsupported accuracy claims.

My Role

Independent planning and AI-assisted build. I completed product planning, information architecture, milestone breakdown, risk controls, interaction design, testing, and iteration management.

AI 体育数据看板

2026 世界杯实时看板

洞察与目标

48 支球队、12 个小组和密集赛程使比赛、积分、赔率与预测信息高度分散。 项目目标是建立一个既能快速扫描、又支持深度探索的统一入口。

策略与规则

将球队、小组、赛程、比赛详情、赔率、预测和 Claude 助手整合到统一信息架构， 以 9 天滚动赛程窗口和每 30 分钟刷新作为核心运营规则。

推进与监控

按数据接入、积分规则、交互展示、预测模块和双语体验拆分里程碑，重点监控接口失效、 数据延迟、规则一致性、预测误差和 API Key 安全；Key 仅保存在本地浏览器。

产出与验证

线上看板 覆盖 48 支球队、12 个小组、9 天赛程窗口和 30 分钟刷新机制。 通过功能覆盖、刷新稳定性、信息可读性和后续预测回测验证价值，不在样本不足时宣称准确率。

我的职责

个人独立策划与 AI 辅助搭建，完成产品规划、信息架构、里程碑拆解、风险控制、交互设计、测试与迭代管理。

Practice Reflection

Across these projects, AI creates value only when it is tied to a clear scenario, operating rules, quality controls, and measurable evidence. My role is to connect business insight, product planning, delivery management, data review, and AI-assisted implementation into workflows that can be tested and improved.
